For years my projects lived in scattered places: commit messages, a paper pad
gone soft at the corners, and my own unreliable memory. This is my attempt to
gather the good parts in one spot.

It's a notebook, not a magazine. I'm not here to lecture, and I'm not going to
pretend the path was straight when it almost never is. When something works
I'll say how. When something bit me — a wrong clock, a backwards connector, an
afternoon lost to a missing ground — I'll say that too, because that's usually
the part that would have saved *you* the afternoon.

A few things I want to keep true here:

- **Short.** A post should be readable while the soldering iron warms up.
- **Concrete.** Real numbers, real parts, real mistakes.
- **Kind.** The blogs I learned the most from were generous with the reader.
  I'd like to pay that forward.

Most of what I build is radio, or the hardware that surrounds it — receivers,
an FPGA HF rig, the odd board to keep the noise where it belongs. Some of it is
finished. Most of it isn't. That's the fun part.

So: welcome. Pull up a stool, and let's make some things work.
